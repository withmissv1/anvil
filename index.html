Project 1

Fill Your Gaps

Your business already has a clear point of view, a defined voice, and named offers, but open-questions.md still contains 40 unanswered decisions, including the audience problem that would make someone pay and the core promise across every offer. Filling those gaps first will turn the structure you already built into reliable instructions for every page, offer, and piece of content that comes next.

You end up with
Real files instead of placeholders.
You learn
A standing rule that makes the folder improve itself.

Full prompt

Check open-questions.md. Tell me how many are still unanswered, then ask me the top one. When I answer, write it into the right file, tick it off, and push to main.

Project 2

Make It Check Your Voice

You end up with
A sharper voice file.
You learn
Your files are a standard to measure against, not just storage.

Full prompt

Read brand/voice.md. Then read this piece of my writing: [paste it]. Go through it and tell me every place it drifts from my voice file. Then tell me what my voice file is missing that would have caught those, add it to the file, and push to main.

Project 3

Let It Play Your Client

You end up with
The questions your messaging cannot answer yet.
You learn
You can hand Claude a role and use it to poke holes in your work.

Full prompt

Read my files, then act as someone who is considering booking [my offer name] and hasn't decided yet. Ask me the questions that person would really ask before paying. One at a time, and wait for my answer. Any time you hit a question my files can't answer, stop and tell me that's a hole in my messaging.

Project 4

A Week of Content From One Recording

You end up with
Actual posts.
You learn
Feeding it your raw material instead of asking it to invent.

Full prompt

Here's a transcript of me talking for a few minutes: [paste it]. Using my voice file and who-i-serve.md, pull out every idea in here worth a post. Give me the list first and wait. Then draft the three strongest in my voice, save them into a content folder, and push to main.

Project 5

Turn a Correction Into a Rule

You end up with
One rule that sticks forever.
You learn
Saying do this beats repeating don't do that.

Full prompt

Here's a correction I keep giving you: [type it]. Rewrite it as a positive instruction that says what to do rather than what to avoid. Add it to CLAUDE.md and push to main.

Project 6

Build One Small Tool

You end up with
A tracker or worksheet that actually works.
You learn
You can make software by describing it.

Full prompt

I want to build [describe the tool] for [who it's for]. Before you write anything, ask me what it needs to give the person at the end. Then build it as one self-contained HTML file with the styling inside it, save it in a folder named after it, and push to main.

Work down the list in order. One project per sitting is enough.
